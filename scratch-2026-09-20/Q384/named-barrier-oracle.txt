Q384 named-barrier correctness trials on Card-A (gfx1201), HIP 7.15 clang 23

Common design:
- 768-thread Q384/KT48 body split into three independent eight-wave groups.
- Each group has a disjoint K/V/scale LDS plane.
- Group waves signal IDs 1/2/3 after staging; consumers wait on their group's ID.
- Candidate metadata: 216 VGPR, 0 VGPR spills, private segment 0, num_named_barrier 3.
