## ADDENDUM: ROCm/HIP portability regrade (2026-07-27)
================================================================================

NInfer is CUDA/sm_120a. NOTHING in it is a paste job onto HIP. Regraded by
transfer class:

### Class A — Pure algorithm/schedule (ports as design; zero ISA dependency)
- RoundState device-side control plane (hipMemcpyAsync / tiny HIP kernels write
  step-IO; tape kernargs hold fixed VAs)
- Frontier-bucketed multi-IB variants + GqaExecutionEnvelope + fail-closed
  coverage validation
- On-device speculative accept -> gdn_initial_slot -> select_hidden chain
  (__shfl exists in HIP; mask semantics differ from *_sync membermask API)
- prepare_masked_block (trivial HIP port)
- Three-regime MoE dispatch + small_t regime (host planner; re-measure
  crossovers on gfx1201, never copy 5090 numbers)
- Folded SwiGLU epilogue, multi-job grouped MMA, nine-warp MoE CTA
  organization (9 x wave32 = 288 threads is legal on gfx11/12 wave32 mode;
  wave64 needs restructure)
- INT8 G64 KV layout + scale factoring ALGORITHM (the factoring math is
  ISA-free; only the MMA substrate below it changes)

### Class B — Near-direct (HIP builtins exist on AMD)
- decode_eight FP16-mantissa dequant: __half2/__hsub2 exist in HIP for AMD;
  constants (0x64006400, bias 1032.0) identical. Cheapest real win.
- __byte_perm -> HIP __byte_perm -> v_perm_b32. Direct.
- exp2 scale-fold, online softmax in exp2: direct.
- sign_extend (x ^ sign) - sign: pure ALU, direct.

### Class C — Re-derive against OUR existing WMMA/MFMA substrate
  (the primitive already lives in kernels/src; the graft is lifting our own
  primitives into a new kernel shape, NOT translating PTX)
- i8 TC attention: wmma_i32_16x16x16_iu8_w32 already proven in
  gemm_hfq4g256_*_mmq; portable part is the structure (on-chip Q quant,
  group-64 rescale epilogue). Fragment layouts differ completely from PTX
  m16n8k32 — do not transliterate.
- ldmatrix -> manual ds_read_b128 fragment packs (we have these).
- GDN WY pipeline: TF32 m16n8k8 has NO AMD equivalent. Use bf16/f16 WMMA
  (must re-validate against the fp32 GDN oracle) or fp32 MFMA on gfx94x only.
  The D->A register-shuffle remap is NVIDIA-fragment-specific: re-derive for
  WMMA lane ownership or replace with an LDS roundtrip.
- mma.sync m16n8k16 bf16 -> WMMA 16x16x16 (gfx11/gfx12 layouts differ from
  each other! half16_t vs half8_t) / MFMA 16x16x16 or 32x32x8 on gfx94x.

### Class D — Restructure (no instruction-level analog on RDNA)
- cp.async rings -> explicit double/triple-buffer: global->VGPR->LDS with
  s_waitcnt vmcnt staging. RDNA3 has no global_load_lds; verify gfx12 status
  before designing around it.
- cp.async.cg streaming loads -> nontemporal glc/slc: KNOWN TRAP — hipfire
  already tried and reverted nontemporal loads once (commit 34eb024).
  Measure first; do not assume the .cg win transfers.
- cudaFuncSetAttribute dynamic-smem + __maxnreg__ occupancy knobs -> no AMD
  analog; use __launch_bounds__ and static LDS budgeting.

### Hard structural constraint found on regrade
- Their i8 prefill smem arena is 92,672 B. RDNA3/RDNA4 LDS is 64 KB per
  workgroup. The 16-warp prefill CTA CANNOT be ported as a shape — strategy
  only, forced re-tile (e.g. 2x8-warp or halved Bc). Same check applies to
  any kernel quoting >64KB smem.
- Persistent grids sized to 170 SMs (5090) -> parameterize from ArchCaps;
  gfx1100=96 CU, gfx1201=64 CU, gfx1151=?. Never hardcode.

### Net effect on ranking
Ranking from the survey stands, but Class A items (control plane, MoE
planner) become even more attractive: they carry zero ISA risk. Class C
items (i8 attention, GDN WY) are where the real engineering budget goes.
Class D pipeline polish drops to last — our own revert history says the
NVIDIA memory-path wins are the least likely to survive contact with RDNA.
