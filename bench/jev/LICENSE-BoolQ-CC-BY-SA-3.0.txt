BoolQ-derived data: licence notice
===================================

The following files hold data derived from BoolQ, under BoolQ's own
licence, and are the one exception to this repository's overall licensing
(see "Scope" below):

  bench/jev/*/probs/jevbench_doc-yesno.jsonl.gz
  bench/jev/*/heldout/doc-yesno.jsonl.gz

Each row is `{"source", "type", "probs", "gold"}`: served probabilities and
a label index (spec `docs/specs/2026-09-28-jev-decide-design.md` §13.6),
with no BoolQ passage or question text and no option keys. See
bench/jev/DATA-LICENSES.md for the full per-dataset licence review.
