VERDICT: The accumulator relief is real: the direct 2x4 wave64 arm compiles at 131 VGPR with zero spills, but is 35.442% slower on gate-up and 26.049% slower on residual. The real 4x4 reuse arm is byte-identical and shares one staged W slab across eight wave64s over a 128x256 macro-tile, but its 198 VGPR footprint limits the compiler to 3 waves/SIMD; an 8-wave block therefore admits only 1 block/CU (2 resident waves/SIMD), not the intended LDS-limited 3 blocks/CU/6 waves/SIMD. It remains 24.928% and 27.917% slower, so accumulator relief does not outweigh occupancy loss. A product kernel would require a distinct wave64 JIT source and host dispatch entry compiled with -mwavefrontsize64, matching 512-thread/20480-byte/128x256 launch geometry, and an explicit Radiowave wave64 request/policy exception; this probe gives no performance basis to add them.
