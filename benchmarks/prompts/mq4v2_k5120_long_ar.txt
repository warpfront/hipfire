Write an original 5000-word educational adventure story in English, divided into twelve numbered chapters. Two students restore an abandoned coastal lighthouse and learn to investigate unreliable measurements. Include realistic dialogue, a mystery involving tides, a mistaken hypothesis corrected by evidence, a simple arithmetic problem that they solve correctly, and a satisfying conclusion. Each chapter must contain at least 400 words and develop a different event; do not summarize future chapters or repeat earlier passages. Begin directly with Chapter 1. Write the full narrative, not an outline or commentary about writing.
