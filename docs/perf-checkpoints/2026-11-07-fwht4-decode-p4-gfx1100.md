## P4b: GQA-shared decode tile (PARKED — correct but slower; occupancy v2 tried, same verdict)

- New kernel (`attention_flash_fwht4_tile_gqa.gfx11.hip`): one 256-thread
  workgroup per (kv head, tile); K tile resident in LDS once (v1) /
  key-partitioned 8×16 straight from global with no K LDS (v2); V in
  16-key (v1) / 32-key (v2) LDS slabs, Q rotated at stage-in; same
  partials ABI (existing reduce).
- Correctness proven: `tmp_fwht_gqa_oracle` (lab-gated) is **bit-identical**
  vs the scalar tile on T=1/2/16/17/32/128/256/66048 × q8/lloyd4 synthetic
  inputs (the 516-tile case reproduces the production 512-wide grid with
  multi-iteration blocks); greedy battery 10/10 vs scalar (both versions).
- Perf v1: long-ctx (92K prompt) decode 28.65 → 16.2 tok/s (**-44%**),
  short 49.1 → 48.0 (-2%).
- Perf v2 (no K LDS, ~17.5 KB → 3 WGs/CU, ~11 barriers/tile instead of
  ~20): short 49.1 → 48.0 (-2%, identical to v1), medium (7.7K prompt,
  `benchmarks/prompts/scan_8k_synth.txt` md5 `510d3c5c…`) 46.6 → 42.5
  (**-9%**, no overlap, sd ≤0.7). The occupancy work did not move the
  needle — and the loss SCALES with ctx (-2% → -9% → -44%), so per-tile
  overhead (occupancy/barriers) was the wrong model; the traffic saving
  itself is not realized (IC absorbs scalar re-reads at short/medium,
  and something else dominates at long). Next step needs device-side
  profiling first, not another blind rewrite.
- Long-ctx re-measurement of v2 BLOCKED environmentally: since ~13:30 the
  box completes no 92K bench on ANY binary (new, old/archived
  `D:/kld/p4a-base`, graph on/off, GQA on/off, max_seq auto/96K) — stuck
  after model load with idle GPU and a spinning daemon, while short and
  medium legs complete normally. Not the kernel (oracle passes at 516
  tiles; batteries green). Suspect driver/KFD-state degradation after a
  day of benchmarking; needs a fresh box (reboot is the operator's call).
  Do not cite v1's long-ctx number as replicable until re-measured.
- PARKED: dispatch arm reverted to a comment; nothing slow ships. Kept
  with the oracle as reference. Debug trail: K staging must gather
  per-head (a contiguous copy reads wrong heads past key 0 — caught by
  the oracle, not by KLD); LDS budget is 64 KB total incl. dynamic;
  entry symbol must match the launcher lookup; cargo misses repo-root
  `.hip` edits (touch + rebuild + md5-verify, twice bitten).
