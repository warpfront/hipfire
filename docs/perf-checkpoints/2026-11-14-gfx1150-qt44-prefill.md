# gfx1150 qt44 (MQ4G256V2) prefill admission — 2026-11-14

**Lifecycle:** `historical`

**Disposition:** measured local A/B on a single `gfx1150` APU. Two stacked
admissions of existing gfx11 kernels, each with its own kill switch. Not a
cross-architecture result, not a product speed floor, not an admission
decision for any other arch, and not a bitwise claim for the W4A4 arm.

**Change under test:** two commits, measured first on top of `89e32856d`
(v0.4.6) and re-measured after the rebase onto beta (`1f0870fde`, the 0.5.0
consolidation line), which is the landing target. Commit subjects, not
hashes (the rebase rewrites them):

- `rdna-compute: admit gfx1150 to the qt44 prefill weight-reuse tiles` —
  adds the gfx1150 `bits=4` rows to `mqv2_prefill_batch_tile` (GateUp 96+ →
  BT12, Qkvza/Qkv/Residual 96+ → BT4, the gfx1151 policy), routes the four
  MQ4V2 prefill dispatchers through a new `Gpu::mqv2_prefill_tile` wrapper,
  and admits `"gfx1151" | "gfx1150"` at their launch sites. Kill switch
  `HIPFIRE_MQ4V2_GFX1150_BT` (default on for exact gfx1150; `0` restores the
  one-tile base WMMA kernel). Unchanged by the rebase: the tile table still
  starts at N = 96 and decode never takes a row.
- `rdna-compute: admit gfx1150 to the qt44 W4A4 prefill block` — replaces
  the four arch-string comparisons `matches!(arch, "gfx1100" | "gfx1151")`
  with `Gpu::mq4v2_mmq_prefill_ok` / `Gpu::mq4v2_iu4_prefill_ok`, and puts
  gfx1150 inside `FeatureFlags::iu4_prefill_enabled()` behind
  `HIPFIRE_MQ4V2_GFX1150_IU4` (default on for exact gfx1150; `0` restores the
  F16-activation WMMA route; the project-wide `HIPFIRE_IU4_PREFILL=0` /
  `kernel.iu4_prefill = false` opt-out now applies to gfx1150 as well).
  gfx1150 runs the generic gfx11 `gemm_mq4g256v2_residual_mmq_iu4` /
  `_full_set_occ3` / `_full_add_occ3` kernels; the gfx1151-only V2B/V2C,
  symfold, X5, LF16 and grid-spec variants stay closed by their own gates.

Rebase consequence (review point 1): on beta the W4A4 block's row gate is
`prefill_rows_admit(batch_size)` (`n >= 64 || (n > 0 &&
ordinary_prefill_active())`), not the master `batch_size >= 64`, so on gfx1150
— as on the certified gfx1100/gfx1151 — a short-prompt prefill chunk inside
ordinary prefill intent now also takes the W4A4 route. The small-N arm below
measures exactly that. Decode (batch 1) is still not admitted: the ordinary
prefill scope is not active at batch 1, so `prefill_rows_admit(1)` is false.

Neither commit adds a kernel, changes a weight layout, or changes any other
architecture's route. Both are default-on **only** for the exact string
`gfx1150`.

## Fixture

- Host: Ryzen AI 9 HX 370 / Radeon 890M, **gfx1150**, 16 CU, 61 GB RAM
  (42.9 GB UMA). ROCm 10.1 / HIP 7.16 / clang 24.
- Model: `qwen3.8-27b.mq4-xts`, 14987185152 bytes, sha256
  `3e38ccbae3776470eb5a89344d300e9279d6b9ab6c31fd40ca1758c4f7c6f8ae`
  (the canonical dense pin from `AGENTS.md` § 5).
- KV: q8 K/V, legacy backend, explicit (`--kv-mode q8 --kv-backend legacy`),
  fully resident, `memory.max_seq` 32768.
- Prompts: `benchmarks/prompts/agentic_pi_system.txt`, 5274 bytes, 1235
  tokens, md5 `2d4892884dfeec9fbddfde2a2f47220f`; and (small-N arm)
  `benchmarks/prompts/agentic_short_extract.txt`, 156 bytes, 46 tokens, md5
  `bb45ee0cb29cff1d84ac781475fd8bfe`. Byte-identical across every process in
  each A/B.
- Invocation: `hipfire bench qwen3.8:27b-mq4-xts --spec off --runs 1
  --warmups 2 --max-tokens 8 --backend noslots --workload stateless
  --kv-mode q8 --kv-backend legacy`. `--spec off` isolates prefill from
  DFlash so τ noise cannot enter the cell.

## Method

6 measured cells per arm (one bench process per cell, arms alternating
on/off per cell after the discarded warmups), median reported, the range in
parentheses is the observed min–max. A/B thresholds were fixed before the
measurement: 15 % was the abandon bar for both commits.

## Result 1 — weight-reuse tiles

Measured on the v0.4.6 base (12 interleaved fresh processes, 6 cells per
arm; `hipfire` md5 `361d00805e7de580d3af4f09b68f42bc`, `daemon` md5
`8c6266e27eaf71d4544eb65e3848608a`):

| arm | prefill (tok/s) | TTFT |
|---|---:|---:|
| `HIPFIRE_MQ4V2_GFX1150_BT=0` (base WMMA) | **67.3** (65.9 – 67.7) | 18.2 s |
| `HIPFIRE_MQ4V2_GFX1150_BT=1` (default) | **117.1** (114.1 – 117.5) | 10.5 s |

**+74 %, no overlap between the arms** (the flag-on minimum 114.1 is above the
flag-off maximum 67.7).

Correctness: greedy (`hipfire run --spec off -t 0 --top-p 1`) prefill-plus-decode
text is **identical** between the arms on the long prompt (111 tokens, same
content md5). One dequantized weight tile feeds `16 * batch_tile` output rows
with the base kernel's per-output WMMA chain, K order and epilogue, so the
arithmetic is unchanged — this one is numerically equivalent, not merely
argmax-equal.

Unit test `gfx1150_qt44_takes_the_gfx1151_bt_rows` pins the new table rows.

## Result 2 — W4A4 / MMQ IU4 block (re-measured on beta)

Measured on top of the tiles (default routing), after the rebase onto beta
(`1f0870fde`) with the review-point-3 gating
(`FeatureFlags::iu4_prefill_enabled()` owns the gfx1150 admission;
binaries: `hipfire` md5 `c0829276414153b4ae293fe1034acab7`, `daemon` md5
`ad0b8b78bf3dfb5f6e6536972e8f8b1e`):

| arm | prefill (tok/s) | TTFT |
|---|---:|---:|
| `HIPFIRE_MQ4V2_GFX1150_IU4=0` (F16-activation WMMA) | **119.65** (119.4 – 119.8) | — |
| `HIPFIRE_MQ4V2_GFX1150_IU4=1` (default, W4A4) | **185.45** (185.3 – 185.8) | — |

**+55 %, no overlap between the arms.** The same A/B on the v0.4.6 base
measured 120.20 / 185.75 tok/s — the rebase (and the `prefill_rows_admit`
row gate) changes nothing at this prompt length.

Decode (batch 1) is unchanged at **6.1 tok/s**: batch 1 is not admitted by
the row policy (no ordinary-prefill intent), so a single-row decode never
reaches the block.

Correctness: this route quantizes the activation to int4, so it is **not**
byte-identical by construction. Greedy output against the WMMA route was
nevertheless unchanged on both fixtures tried at ≥ 64 rows (`hipfire run
--spec off -t 0 --top-p 1`, 27B qt44):

- `benchmarks/prompts/merge_sort_thinking_off.txt` — 183 tokens, 530 chars,
  md5 `927f70ef48e9…`
- `benchmarks/prompts/bare_factual.txt` — 300 tokens, 599 chars, md5
  `4be06b5a059eec61`

The int4 activation step does not move the argmax on these prompts. This is
**not** a bitwise claim. Below 64 rows it is not even argmax-stable — see
Result 3.

## Result 3 — small-N admission (46 tokens, the rebase consequence)

Fixture `benchmarks/prompts/agentic_short_extract.txt` (46 tokens, ordinary
prefill intent → admitted). The `IU4=0` arm is the base WMMA kernel here,
because the BT tiles still require N ≥ 96.

| arm | prefill (tok/s) |
|---|---:|
| `HIPFIRE_MQ4V2_GFX1150_IU4=0` (base WMMA) | **75.15** (75.0 – 75.3) |
| `HIPFIRE_MQ4V2_GFX1150_IU4=1` (default, W4A4) | **72.35** (72.2 – 72.6) |

**−3.7 % prefill, no overlap between the arms** — about 24 µs absolute on a
TTFT of well under a second (the bench flags this length as dominated by
launch overhead). W4A4 is a small loss at this extent, not a win, and the
greedy argmax **moves**: the two arms agree through the shared prefix
"…If you could paste the contents of `/tmp/" and then diverge (the
`IU4=1` arm stops at "`/tmp/`", the `IU4=0` arm continues
"`quotes.txt` here, I'd be happy"). This is the expected int4-activation
behaviour at tiny extents and is disclosed rather than gated around: the
admission deliberately gives gfx1150 the same row policy as the certified
gfx1100/gfx1151 routes.

## Quality — KLD against the certified A4 route

`eval_hipfire --scoring-mode prefill --max-chunks 24` on the canonical
`qwen3.8:27b.mq4-xts` pin, `HIPFIRE_GRAPH=0 HIPFIRE_NORMALIZE_PROMPT=0`,
fresh HOME/kernel cache per run, kv q8, references from
`fixtures/pins.toml` (the same prompt set as the gfx1100/gfx1151 A4
admissions). Two repeats per arm; the eval is deterministic (identical
values per arm across repeats).

| reference | gfx1150 IU4=1 (W4A4) | certified gfx1100/gfx1151 A4 pin | gfx1150 IU4=0 (WMMA+BT) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `qwen3.8-27b.ref_wt2.bin` (sha256 `8c545178…43234a`) | **0.068198** | 0.069955 | 0.039226 |
| `heldout-code24.gptqsym.kldref.bin` (sha256 `43ab7591…02ec7582`) | **0.050419** | 0.054395 | 0.027072 |

The gfx1150 W4A4 route is slightly **better** than the certified
gfx1100/gfx1151 A4 route on both pins (−2.5 % KLD on WT2, −7.3 % on
code24), which is what defaulting it on for gfx1150 rests on. The
F16-activation WMMA route remains the quality ceiling (roughly half the
KLD), and `HIPFIRE_MQ4V2_GFX1150_IU4=0` / `HIPFIRE_IU4_PREFILL=0` are the
route back to it. KLD values compare against pins; kldseq hashes are
host/arch-specific and are not claimed here.

## Stacked effect (1235-token prompt)

| configuration | prefill (tok/s) | vs base |
|---|---:|---:|
| base (both flags off) | 67.3 | 1.00× |
| + BT tiles | 117.1 | 1.74× |
| + BT tiles + W4A4 | 185.45 | **2.76×** |

TTFT on the 1235-token prompt falls from 18.2 s to 6.7 s at the configured
32k context. The two gains are additive because they remove different costs:
the batch tile removes redundant weight dequantization per output row, the
W4A4 block removes the F16 activation traffic and the unpack-bound WMMA
chain.

## Why gfx1150 was compute-bound

At the base route, gfx1150 prefill measured 123.9 / 117.1 / 114.5 tok/s at
512 / 1235 / 4907 prompt tokens (−7.6 % over a 9.5× prompt length), i.e.
~6.2 TFLOP/s — 13–18 % of the 16-CU WMMA f16 peak, flat in prompt length.
That signature is compute-bound, not bandwidth-bound, which is exactly what
the weight-reuse tile and W4A4 address. The flatness also rules out a KV
capacity or placement effect.

## Caveats

- Single host, single model, single prompt, single quant format. The numbers
  are bound to the fixture above and are not transferable to another GPU,
  prompt shape, quant or context length without a new record.
- Only `MQ4G256V2` (qt44) prefill is affected, over the row extents the
  ordinary prefill row-admission policy admits (any positive extent inside
  ordinary prefill intent, N ≥ 64 outside). Decode, MQ3/MQ6/MQ8, and the
  gfx1150 MoE (`MQ4G256V2` expert) prefill path are out of scope; the MoE
  prefill has no MMQ/i8 twin on any architecture and is a separate piece of
  work.
- The small-N arm (Result 3) shows W4A4 is a small prefill loss and an
  argmax mover below 64 rows; it is kept because it is the certified arches'
  own policy, and the kill switches are the documented route back.
- No served-path measurement is recorded here. The user-facing serve route
  (`hipfire bench --backend slots`, continuous batching) was not measured for
  this change; the claim is a prefill cell, not a served win.
- The two arms are interleaved fresh processes but both run on the same APU
  with its shared UMA and DPM behaviour; the reported ranges are the observed
  spread, not a bootstrap CI.
