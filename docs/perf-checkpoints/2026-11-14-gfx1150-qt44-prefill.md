# gfx1150 qt44 (MQ4G256V2) prefill admission — 2026-11-14

**Lifecycle:** `historical`

**Disposition:** measured local A/B on a single `gfx1150` APU. Two stacked
admissions of existing gfx11 kernels, each with its own kill switch. Not a
cross-architecture result, not a product speed floor, not an admission
decision for any other arch, and not a bitwise claim for the W4A4 arm.

**Change under test:** two commits on top of `89e32856d` (v0.4.6).

- `73291bfc0` — `rdna-compute: admit gfx1150 to the qt44 prefill weight-reuse
  tiles`. Adds the gfx1150 `bits=4` rows to `mqv2_prefill_batch_tile`
  (GateUp 96+ → BT12, Qkvza/Qkv/Residual 96+ → BT4, the gfx1151 policy),
  routes the four MQ4V2 prefill dispatchers through a new
  `Gpu::mqv2_prefill_tile` wrapper, and admits `"gfx1151" | "gfx1150"` at
  their launch sites. Kill switch `HIPFIRE_MQ4V2_GFX1150_BT` (default on for
  exact gfx1150; `0` restores the one-tile base WMMA kernel).
- `d65f6c420` — `rdna-compute: admit gfx1150 to the qt44 W4A4 prefill block`.
  Replaces the four arch-string comparisons `matches!(arch, "gfx1100" |
  "gfx1151")` (N ≥ 64) and the four inner `iu4_prefill_enabled()` conditions
  with `Gpu::mq4v2_mmq_prefill_ok` / `Gpu::mq4v2_iu4_prefill_ok`, and lets
  `gemm_mq4g256v2_mmq_prequant_iu4`'s guard accept gfx1150 behind the same
  flag. Kill switch `HIPFIRE_MQ4V2_GFX1150_IU4` (default on for exact gfx1150;
  `0` restores the F16-activation WMMA route). gfx1150 runs the generic gfx11
  `gemm_mq4g256v2_residual_mmq_iu4` / `_full_set_occ3` / `_full_add_occ3`
  kernels; the gfx1151-only V2B/V2C, symfold, X5, LF16 and grid-spec variants
  stay closed by their own gates.

Neither commit adds a kernel, changes a weight layout, or changes any other
architecture's route. Both are default-on **only** for the exact string
`gfx1150`.

## Fixture

- Host: Ryzen AI 9 HX 370 / Radeon 890M, **gfx1150**, 16 CU, 61 GB RAM
  (42.9 GB UMA). ROCm 10.1 / HIP 7.16 / clang 24.
- Model: `qwen3.8-27b.mq4-xts`, 14987185152 bytes, sha256
  `3e38ccbae3776470eb5a89344d300e9279d6b9ab6c31fd40ca1758c4f7c6f8ae`
  (the canonical dense pin from `AGENTS.md` § 5).
- KV: q8 K/V, legacy backend (gfx1150 has no VMM/FP8 KV route), fully
  resident, `memory.max_seq` 32768.
- Prompt: `benchmarks/prompts/agentic_pi_system.txt`, 5274 bytes, 1235
  tokens, md5 `2d4892884dfeec9fbddfde2a2f47220f`. Byte-identical across every
  process in both A/Bs.
- Invocation: `hipfire bench qwen3.8:27b-mq4-xts --spec off --runs 1
  --warmups 1 --max-tokens 8 --backend noslots --workload stateless`.
  `--spec off` isolates prefill from DFlash so τ noise cannot enter the cell.

## Method

12 interleaved fresh processes per arm (flag off / flag on alternating, one
load per process), 6 measured cells per arm after the discarded warmup.
Reported value is the median of the six `prefill_tok_s.median` values; the
range in parentheses is the observed min–max. A/B thresholds were fixed
before the measurement: 15 % was the abandon bar for both commits.

## Result 1 — weight-reuse tiles (`73291bfc0`)

| arm | prefill (tok/s) | TTFT |
|---|---:|---:|
| `HIPFIRE_MQ4V2_GFX1150_BT=0` (base WMMA) | **67.3** (65.9 – 67.7) | 18.2 s |
| `HIPFIRE_MQ4V2_GFX1150_BT=1` (default) | **117.1** (114.1 – 117.5) | 10.5 s |

**+74 %, no overlap between the arms** (the flag-on minimum 114.1 is above the
flag-off maximum 67.7).

Binaries: `hipfire` md5 `361d00805e7de580d3af4f09b68f42bc`, `daemon` md5
`8c6266e27eaf71d4544eb65e3848608a`.

Correctness: greedy (`hipfire run --spec off -t 0 --top-p 1`) prefill-plus-decode
text is **identical** between the arms on the long prompt (111 tokens, same
content md5). One dequantized weight tile feeds `16 * batch_tile` output rows
with the base kernel's per-output WMMA chain, K order and epilogue, so the
arithmetic is unchanged — this one is numerically equivalent, not merely
argmax-equal.

Unit test `gfx1150_qt44_takes_the_gfx1151_bt_rows` pins the new table rows.

## Result 2 — W4A4 / MMQ IU4 block (`d65f6c420`)

Measured on top of result 1 (default routing).

| arm | prefill (tok/s) | TTFT |
|---|---:|---:|
| `HIPFIRE_MQ4V2_GFX1150_IU4=0` (F16-activation WMMA) | **120.20** (119.5 – 120.3) | — |
| `HIPFIRE_MQ4V2_GFX1150_IU4=1` (default, W4A4) | **185.75** (185.6 – 186.3) | — |

**+54.5 %, no overlap between the arms.**

Decode (batch 1) is unchanged at **6.1 tok/s**: the block requires N ≥ 64, so
a single-row decode never reaches it.

Binaries: `hipfire` md5 `183a947884b9e87797c09d098e46843a`, `daemon` md5
`0371679bab389a179463fba7211a25b2`.

Correctness: this route quantizes the activation to int4, so it is **not**
byte-identical by construction. Greedy output against the WMMA route was
nevertheless unchanged on both fixtures tried (`hipfire run --spec off -t 0
--top-p 1`, 27B qt44):

- `benchmarks/prompts/merge_sort_thinking_off.txt` — 183 tokens, 530 chars,
  md5 `927f70ef48e9…`
- `benchmarks/prompts/bare_factual.txt` — 300 tokens, 599 chars, md5
  `4be06b5a059eec61`

The int4 activation step does not move the argmax on these prompts. This is
**not** a bitwise claim.

## Stacked effect

| configuration | prefill (tok/s) | vs base |
|---|---:|---:|
| base (both flags off) | 67.3 | 1.00× |
| + BT tiles | 117.1 | 1.74× |
| + BT tiles + W4A4 | 185.75 | **2.76×** |

TTFT on the 1235-token prompt falls from 18.2 s to 6.8 s at the configured
32k context. The two gains are additive because they remove different costs:
the batch tile removes redundant weight dequantization per output row, the
W4A4 block removes the F16 activation traffic and the unpack-bound WMMA
chain.

## Why gfx1150 was compute-bound

At the base route, gfx1150 prefill measured 123.9 / 117.1 / 114.5 tok/s at
512 / 1235 / 4907 prompt tokens (−7.6 % over a 9.5× prompt length), i.e.
~6.2 TFLOP/s — 13–18 % of the 16-CU WMMA f16 peak, flat in prompt length.
That signature is compute-bound, not bandwidth-bound, which is exactly what
the weight-reuse tile and W4A4 address. The flatness also rules out a KV
capacity or placement effect.

## Caveats

- Single host, single model, single prompt, single quant format. The numbers
  are bound to the fixture above and are not transferable to another GPU,
  prompt shape, quant or context length without a new record.
- Only `MQ4G256V2` (qt44) and `N ≥ 64` prefill are affected. Decode,
  MQ3/MQ6/MQ8, and the gfx1150 MoE (`MQ4G256V2` expert) prefill path are out
  of scope; the MoE prefill has no MMQ/i8 twin on any architecture and is a
  separate piece of work.
- No served-path measurement is recorded here. The user-facing serve route
  (`hipfire bench --backend slots`, continuous batching) was not measured for
  this change; the claim is a prefill cell, not a served win.
- The two arms are interleaved fresh processes but both run on the same APU
  with its shared UMA and DPM behaviour; the reported ranges are the observed
  spread, not a bootstrap CI.
