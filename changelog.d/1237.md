### Fixes: Inkling-2-Mini long-context tau scaling

- **Fixed (#1237, Inkling-2-Mini, arch 24): full-attention visits now apply the
  model's Q/R tau scaling past position 8192 instead of refusing the step.**
  Admission accepts finite, positive tau values through the supported bound,
  and the shared pre-attention path applies the same scaling in eager decode
  and blocked prefill. Capture keeps unscaled `qc`/`rc` keys and adds separate
  scaled keys for parity gates. `HIPFIRE_INKLING2_TAU_OFF=1` remains the
  documented developer kill switch for the ON/OFF gate arm. Contributed by
  raufaser.
