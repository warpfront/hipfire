### Fixes

- **Fixed (retained PM4 replay, gfx1201 and every other PM4 target): a transient KFD queue eviction no longer fails the turn at the 5 s bound.** KFD evicts all of a process's queues while it migrates or compacts host pages the runtime registered with the GPU (kcompactd bursts on userptr BOs); a replay in flight stalls for the whole eviction (episodes up to 2.2 s measured, `kcompactd0`/`pgmigrate` rho 0.87, evicted stall time vs extra step time r=0.999), and the fixed `DEFAULT_WAIT_TIMEOUT` turned an unlucky tail into `HIPFIRE_REDLINE_QUEUE_TIMEOUT` ("HSA signal ... did not complete within 5 s", the gfx1201 lane flake) where HIP just waits. `SingleQueuePm4Ib::wait_replay_checked` now reads the process's `/sys/class/kfd/kfd/proc/<pid>/stats_*/evicted_ms` (summed) only once a wait has outlived 250 ms and again when the deadline expires; while it keeps rising the deadline is extended by one 5 s window per rising window, up to a 60 s ceiling, with one `HIPFIRE_REDLINE_EVICT_WAIT_EXTENDED evicted_ms_delta=... extension_ms=... deadline_ms=...` line per extension. A flat or unreadable counter (no sysfs, non-Linux) fails at exactly 5 s as before, a queue fault still ends the wait at once, and the normal path never touches sysfs. Deterministic unit tests drive the loop with a simulated counter and clock (rising, flat, unavailable, rising forever to the ceiling). `HSA_USERPTR_FOR_PAGED_MEM=0` still avoids the evictions altogether. See `docs/REDLINE.md` "Bounded replay wait and KFD eviction".
