### Changes

- **Perf: Inkling-2-Mini (arch 24) plain-AR decode runs a retained tape instead of 1,024 eager launches per token.** The frozen one-row decode body (one H2D, the 48-visit body and head, one D2H) is recorded once per context bucket and replayed: as a retained PM4 tape by default on the admitted row (gfx1201, the Mini fixed schedule, the typed-XTS certificate, A8 experts with the fused producer recipe, F16 KV, FA2 attention, one device, no drafter), as a HIP graph under `HIPFIRE_REPLAY_BACKEND=hip` and as the fail-closed fallback when a PM4 record or prepare is refused, and as the incumbent eager decode under the existing kill switches `HIPFIRE_GRAPH=0` / `HIPFIRE_AR_GRAPH=0` or for any row outside the admitted one. Logits and per-visit KV/conv/tag state are bit-identical to the eager decode on every compared step (bucket crossings, SWA wrap, resets), greedy token ids and seeded sampling streams are identical across the three routes, and prompt prefill (including a one-row tail) never selects a retained route. No new environment flag. `daemon` `bench_decode` now serves arch 24 through the same wrapper and reports which backend ran; `scripts/redline_daemon_harness.py --inkling2` proves the route per arm.
