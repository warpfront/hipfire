### Changes

- **Perf: F16 GEMVs on gfx12 (1..=16 rows through `gemm_f16_batched_lmhead`) run `gemm_f16_wmma_n16_gfx12`, bit-identical to `gemm_f16_wmma_mb8_gfx12`, ~10x faster.** The mb8 kernel computed eight 16-column WMMA tiles per block (seven dead at N <= 16) and waited on eight scalar F16 loads before every WMMA: 97 us for the Inkling-2-Mini `mq4-xts` F16 router (M 128, K 2304, one row) on an R9700, 4.5 ms of every decode token over 48 visits, which made the typed XTS artifact 3-6% slower than the shipped RTN one. The new symbol (same module) issues the same `wmma_f32_16x16x16_f16` chain on the same operands (X rounded to F16 by the same conversion, same per-lane mapping, ascending K), drops the dead tiles and fetches 16-byte operand vectors 16 steps ahead: 9.4 us at the router shape, 15 us at M 2304 x K 3072. Admitted for gfx12 WMMA, `k % 16 == 0`, 16-byte aligned operands; anything else keeps mb8. Gate: `rdna-compute/tests/gemm_f16_wmma_n16_hw.rs` (bitwise vs mb8 for every N in 1..=16 over router, ragged-M, short-K and wide shapes); Inkling-2 C WT2 chunk logits digests unchanged.
