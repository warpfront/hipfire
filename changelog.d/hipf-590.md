### Fixes: G12 engine loop, Qwen4 MTP window lanes

- **A retired Qwen4 MTP lane keeps its owed head rows, so its next turn continues live (HIPF-590; `generation.engine_loop = engine` with `serve.vmm_batch`).** Every MTP lane store on the whole-model scope now holds owed-row storage (`HeadLagStore`, 1024 rows, as the singleton's `head_lag`), also without the copy lane. A lane that the floor retires owes its head rows; the root flushes them at capacity, and `unpark` appends them before the record continues. A promoted request that the singleton floor retired catches its head up before the promotion. Before, a retired lane was head-free and `unpark` refused its record, so the next turn prefilled cold and was not token-exact with k=1.
- **Aborting a request that decodes in a Qwen4 MTP window lane keeps its conversation (HIPF-590, the MTP part of HIPF-594).** An MTP park now also takes the turn's resident snapshot into the lane; the snapshot holds the MTP head, so `unpark` rewinds trunk and head to the turn start. Halo, Qwen4 mq4-xts MTP, `engine_loop_ab.py --batch-only`: the decode-abort retry `D1r` reuses 82/82 (was 0/82) with ids equal to `D~1`.
- **Retired Qwen4 MTP lanes queue copies of owed hidden rows without a host wait per lane.** The copies use the same stream as the forward and head append. The existing step fence still checks completion.
