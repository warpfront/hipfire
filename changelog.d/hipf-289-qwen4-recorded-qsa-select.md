### Changes

- **Perf (Qwen4 retained decode tape on exact gfx1151; selected bytes identical): a recorder now takes the PM QSA score/select pair, not the fused capacity-bound selector (HIPF-289).** The pair was eager-only, so a recorded tape ran `indexed_attention_select_f32_batched` over the shape bound (`pooled_capacity` blocks) at every position. Under a recorder the pair now scores over the shape bound with the block count and `position_start` declared to the recorder (`PositionDivU32` / `PositionPlusU32`), the score grid declares `PositionCeilDiv` (used under `HIPFIRE_REPLAY_PM4_DYNAMIC_GRID`), and the tape reads the F32 pooled keys, never the BF16 mirror (its converter does not run under a recorder). The eager pair reserves the shape-bound score row on every arch, so a recording finds the scratch already sized. Per call, Flash-Next shape (1 row, 4x128 index, compress 4, budget 2048, 4096-block bound), recorded-HIP replay on gfx1151: 54.4 -> 11.6 us at 828 blocks, 77.7 -> 13.7 us at 2475 blocks, 100.6 -> 18.9 us at 4096 blocks; 4.1 -> 7.7 us at 5 blocks. With 12 QSA layers that is about 0.5 ms per token at 3.3K context and 0.8 ms at 9.9K. Other archs keep their recorded routes. The beta transport gate still refuses the Qwen4 tape on gfx11 PM4 (HIPF-254), so default loads do not change.
