### Changes

- **Inkling-2-Mini: `hipfire-quantize --format inkling2-dspark-bf16 --input <checkpoint dir> --base <trunk.hfq>` packs the bundled DSpark draft (`mtp.safetensors`) into a `<trunk-stem>-dspark.hfq` sidecar, and `hipfire_arch_inkling2::InklingDsparkWeights::load` reads it.** The sidecar keeps the 54 source tensors' names and BF16 bytes and adds the draft's `hidden_out` projection folded into the paired trunk's unembed. It is bound to that one trunk (legacy or `qt44-padded-awq-v1` XTS): source repo and revision, source trunk and draft checkpoint digests, tokenizer, loop schedule, trunk layout and the embed, embed-norm and unembed bytes. The reader refuses a sidecar that differs from its manifest, its trunk or its fold. Nothing loads the sidecar yet: Inkling still runs AR, and loading a trunk without a sidecar is unchanged.
