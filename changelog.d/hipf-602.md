### Fixes: G12 engine loop, Qwen4 AR resident cancels

- **Aborting a Qwen4 AR request that decodes on the resident state keeps its conversation (HIPF-602; `generation.engine_loop = engine` with `serve.vmm_batch`).** A resident cancel ran the attested rollback, which resets every owner, so the next turn of another conversation took the resident state and the aborted conversation was lost: a retry reused 0 tokens. A resident AR cancel after its prompt (a decode abort, or a client abort of a finished turn on `commit_ready`) now parks the state into a lane and keeps it as the record of its turn start, as a lane cancel does (HIPF-594). A prompt-phase abort and a speculative resident request still roll back. Halo, Qwen4 mq4-xts AR, Loki's `loki599.py race1,race2` (abort of A1 with B1 sent at the same time): the retry `A1r` reuses 82/82 (was 0/82) with ids equal to `A~1`.
- **Engine-loop A/B harness: the batch scenario covers a resident abort followed by another conversation (HIPF-602).** `scripts/engine_loop_ab.py --batch` adds two steps: D's turn 2 alone (resident), aborted after 8 tokens with P's turn 2 sent right behind the abort; then D's and D~'s turn 2. P2, D~2 and D2r must reuse their committed turn 1, and D2r must match D~2. Halo: the AR route passes (D2r and D~2 179/179; the daemon before the fix gives D~2 0/179); the MTP route fails on D~2 0/179, the same defect on speculative resident requests (HIPF-608).
