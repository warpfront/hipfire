### 0.5.0 consolidation

- **Changed: the generated half of every crate `map.md` is no longer committed.** `scripts/check-crate-maps.py` (called by `cargo xtask regen`) now writes the module/export/dependency tables to `crates/<name>/map.generated.md`, which is git-ignored; `map.md` keeps its hand-written text and a two-line pointer between the existing markers. One commit in five used to touch a `map.md` only to refresh that block. `check-crate-maps.py --check` (a `ci-gates.sh` step, unchanged) now fails on a `map.md` whose marker span is not the pointer, on a broken local link, or when the tables cannot be produced; it no longer compares generated content, which cannot drift once it is not committed. A fresh checkout has no `map.generated.md` until `cargo xtask regen` runs. New gauge `gauge_committed_generated_files` (plan T6): 79 before, 18 after.
