### Changes

- **Inkling-2-Mini (arch 24) now has a paired KLD evaluator and baseline pins (`hipfire-loader/examples/inkling_kld.rs`).** `eval_hipfire` refuses arch 24, and the published Inkling numbers were a single token-0 KL. `inkling_kld ref` builds HFKLDR v3 references from the published checkpoint (NVFP4/MXFP8 experts dequantized by the quantizer's strict decoders). It runs them through a token-batched CPU forward that is bit-identical to `forward_token_cached` on every hidden state and logit row checked (`inkling_kld fidelity`, including SWA eviction and tau != 1). `eval` scores a candidate through the product loader and `forward_token_gpu`, one fresh process per chunk. `pair` reports per-chunk deltas with paired-t and bootstrap 95% CIs, so FA2 decode attention and a symmetric expert artifact can each be gated against the RTN baseline. Pins: WT2 and code24 at 24 x 2048 (`pin:reference/inkling2-src-wt2-24x2048.v3.kldref`, `pin:reference/inkling2-src-code24-24x2048.v3.kldref`). The RTN baselines `pin:kld/gfx1201.inkling2-mq4.wt2` and `pin:kld/gfx1201.inkling2-mq4.code24` each reproduced byte for byte in two fresh runs on different R9700 cards.
