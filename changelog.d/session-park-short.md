### Changes

- **Flash-Next (Qwen4) keeps a short conversation across an unrelated request: the session cache now parks any conversation whose park copies at most 8192 rows per stream, not only one longer than the side request.** A side request that diverged early from the committed conversation parked it only when the conversation was longer than the side's write bound (prompt + `max_tokens` + lookahead; about 4.1K rows for a request that omits `max_tokens` under the default `generation.max_tokens` 4096). A shorter conversation, which below one prefill chunk (4096 rows on gfx1201) has no snapshot by default, was re-prefilled from row zero, and its greedy continuation could change because the rebuilt state came from prefill instead of decode. The bound is now the rows the park copies, `min(conversation, side bound) <= 8192`, so a shorter conversation is parked whole and its next turn continues it byte-exactly (restored == uninterrupted). R9700 (gfx1201), `qwen3.8-flash-next-base.mq4-xts-symtrunk` single card, AR greedy, 6 fresh loads per arm, bootstrap 95% CI: conversation turn 2 after a 24-token unrelated request 245.6 → 166.0 ms TTFT (0.676× [0.668, 0.683]); in the committed tool-call chain with an unrelated request (no `max_tokens`, `generation.max_tokens` 1024) after every reply, the first reply after one 1004.9 → 807.3 ms (0.803× [0.803, 0.804]), and the chain keeps the uninterrupted transcript byte for byte (it diverged before). Single-conversation TTFT and decode are unchanged (chain TTFT 1.000× [0.999, 1.000], decode 1.000× [0.995, 1.006]); a fresh conversation after another pays the park copy, +0.7 to +1.1 ms TTFT (1.004–1.006×) and about 260 MiB of transient VRAM. `HIPFIRE_SESSION_PARK_SHORT=0` restores the old rule. Message-end snapshots (`HIPFIRE_QWEN4_TURN_SNAPSHOTS`) stay off by default: on the same card they split every prefill at each `<|im_end|>`, costing +28 to +48% TTFT on short cold prompts and +16% across the chain and holding up to 1.7 GiB more VRAM, while restoring less than parking does.
