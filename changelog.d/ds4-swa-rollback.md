### Fixes

- **Fixed (DeepSeek V4 speculative decode: DSpark, on by default when the `-dspark` sidecar is present, and explicit native MTP): a rejected draft no longer corrupts the committed context.** The verify writes every row before acceptance, and two DeepSeek V4 caches are finite rings. Past 128 tokens, a rejected row at `p + j` (`j >= accept + 2`) overwrote the SWA slot of position `p + j - 128`, which the next token still attends. A rejected row that closed a compressor window shifted the ratio-4 overlap ring, and later rows overwrote committed rows of the open window (ratio 4 and 128); on the batched aligned route the committed rows of that window never reached the ring at all. `commit_prefix` (and the native MTP step) now restores both. Before the verify, the SWA columns of rows `>= 2` and the rings of every compressor whose window a draft row closes are saved with kernel copies, and the forward stashes those compressors' rows. On a partial accept the rejected SWA columns are restored and each compressor ring a rejected row closed is rebuilt from its saved state plus the committed rows. A fully accepted window is left as the forward wrote it.
