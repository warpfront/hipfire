### Fixes: speculative context fit

- **Fixed (#1225, Qwen speculative decode with the prompt cache, e.g. dense 27B MTP): a cached-history turn with an omitted `max_tokens` is no longer refused with `context_length` (`prompt+max_tokens exceeds ctx_capacity`).** The spec entry fitted the omitted budget and chose spec vs AR on the cold re-render of the conversation, then ran on the spliced render, which replays the exact tokens earlier turns generated and can be longer; once it was more than about 61 tokens longer the in-loop guard refused the request. The fit, the admitted-context refusal and the spec-vs-AR fallback now use the final prompt. Both speculative capacity refusals now name their counts (`prompt=P + max_tokens=M + block_size=B exceeds ctx_capacity C`).
