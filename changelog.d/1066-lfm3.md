### New model: LFM3 family (arch 26)

- **Added: LiquidAI LFM3 text, VL, audio (ASR) and omni (TTS) on arch 26 (#1066,
  mikiadev).** New crates `hipfire-arch-lfm3` (text backbone: hybrid conv +
  full-attention + sliding-attention, partial RoPE, sandwich norm) and the
  `-vl` / `-audio` / `-omni` tower crates, loaded by the `Lfm3Carrier`; the towers
  are detected by tensor presence on the one arch id. Text generates on the
  `lfm_ar` route and images on the LFM3-VL vision route; audio input goes through
  `input_audio` / `audio_base64`; the omni depth decoder serves `POST /tts`
  (24 kHz WAV) and WebSocket voice sessions on `GET /v1/realtime`. The omni codec
  is read from `HIPFIRE_OMNI_CODEC_DIR` (the checkpoint's `audio_tokenizer/`
  directory). HFQ files quantized before this release carry arch 24 or 13 in their
  header and must be re-quantized.
- **LFM3 limits, refused cleanly.** `/v1/realtime` closes with 1009 a WebSocket
  message larger than `serve.max_request_bytes` (and with 1002 an unmasked
  client frame) and bounds the buffered input audio by the same limit. Audio
  input is refused when the WAV has no complete sample frame, a sample rate
  outside 1 kHz to 384 kHz, more than 600 s of audio, or more audio tokens than
  the context holds. LFM3-VL images are downscaled to at most 1,048,576 px
  (override with `HIPFIRE_VL_MAX_PIXELS`, at most HF's 16,777,216), and an image
  whose visual tokens do not fit the context is refused before the vision tower
  runs. Every LFM3 route polls client aborts before each prefill and decode
  token (and before the vision tower and the audio encoder), and omni ends
  through the commit handshake.
- **LFM3 known gaps.** The LFM3 routes ignore `seed`, `min_tokens`, the repeat
  and presence penalties, `top_k`, `min_p`, `logprobs` and `max_think_tokens`.
  Prefill runs one token at a time. The VL prompt adds a newline after
  `<|vision_end|>` that HF's template does not, and safetensors-directory loads
  use `<|endoftext|>` as BOS where HF uses `<|startoftext|>`. The vision tower's
  attention is O(N²) in the patch count.
