### Features: Inkling-2-Mini blocked prefill

- **Added (#1235, Inkling-2-Mini, arch 24): blocked multi-row prefill and
  load-resident decode scratch.** The F16 blocked path batches QKV/R, attention,
  gate/output projections and per-row routing while keeping the single-row
  fallback for unsupported or one-row blocks. The typed `mq4-xts` layout keeps
  its per-expert AWQ route in both decode and prefill. Relative bias now uses
  the zero-term rule past `rel_extent`, and load/runtime failure paths release
  partial GPU allocations instead of leaking them. Contributed by raufaser.
